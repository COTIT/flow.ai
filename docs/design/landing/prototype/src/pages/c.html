01 | How it works

Open the passport. The whole journey is inside.

Batch passport
  1. HiveBees make the honey. The farmer records each hive.
  2. HarvestEvery harvest is logged: who, where, how much.
  3. PassportPacked into a batch; every jar gets a QR passport.
  4. ScanA buyer scans the jar and reads its story.
  5. SoldOnline or at the till, from one stock list.
  6. Paid backThe farmer’s share flows back into their passbook.

03 | Our promise

Plain rules, kept by the system — not by goodwill.

  1. Private

    Each organisation sees only its own farmers, stock and sales.

  2. Consent

    Data moves to a partner only through a link the farmer agreed to.

  3. Exact

    Money counted to the cent. Passbook lines stay as they were written.
