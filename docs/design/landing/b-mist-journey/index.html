How it works

Five stops from the hive to your kitchen.

  1. Harvest

    Recorded in the field app, even offline: who, where, how much.

  2. Batch

    Many harvests become one batch. Every kilo accounted for.

  3. Passport

    A QR code per batch. Anyone can scan it.

  4. Sale

    Web shop, market or store till — one stock list.

  5. Passbook

    The farmer’s share is written down at sale. Lines never change.
