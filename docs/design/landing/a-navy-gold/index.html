How it works

One jar, five steps, nothing hidden.

  1. Harvest recorded

    In the field app, even without signal: who harvested, where, and how much.

  2. Batch packed

    Harvests from many farmers become one batch. Every kilo is accounted for.

  3. Passport printed

    Each batch gets a QR code. Scan it to see where the jar came from.

  4. Sold anywhere

    Web shop, market stall or store till — one stock list for all of them.

  5. Farmer paid

    Each sale adds a line to the farmer’s passbook. Lines are never changed afterwards.

What we promise

Plain rules, kept by the system.

01

Your account is private

Each organisation sees only its own farmers, stock and sales. Nobody else can look in.

02

Sharing needs consent

Data moves between organisations only through a supply link the farmer agreed to.

03

Money is exact

Every amount is counted to the cent and every passbook line stays as it was written.
