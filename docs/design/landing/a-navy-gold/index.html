How it works

Three gold lines: goods go out, the story travels, money comes back.

  1. HiveBees make the honey. The farmer records each hive.
  2. HarvestEvery harvest is logged: who, where, how much.
  3. PassportPacked into a batch; every jar gets a QR passport.
  4. ScanA buyer scans the jar and reads its story.
  5. SoldOnline or at the till, from one stock list.
  6. Paid backThe farmer’s share flows back into their passbook.

What we promise

Plain rules, kept by the system.

01

Your account is private

Each organisation sees only its own farmers, stock and sales. Nobody else can look in.

02

Sharing needs consent

Data moves between organisations only through a supply link the farmer agreed to.

03

Money is exact

Every amount is counted to the cent and every passbook line stays as it was written.
