01 | How it works

Five steps, one record, nothing re-typed.

  1. Harvest

    Logged in the field app, even without signal: who, where, how much.

  2. Batch

    Harvests from many farmers become one batch. Every kilo accounted for.

  3. Passport

    The batch gets a QR code for its label. Anyone can scan it.

  4. Sale

    Web shop, market stall or store till — all from one stock list.

  5. Passbook

    The farmer’s share is written down at sale. Lines are never changed.

03 | Our promise

Plain rules, kept by the system — not by goodwill.

  1. Private

    Each organisation sees only its own farmers, stock and sales.

  2. Consent

    Data moves to a partner only through a link the farmer agreed to.

  3. Exact

    Money counted to the cent. Passbook lines stay as they were written.
