The harvest

The harvest starts in June when the cherries turn deep red. Families pick only the ripe ones, then spread them on raised beds made of mesh so air reaches every side. After about three weeks the cherries are hulled at the Ilundo store and weighed — each farmer’s share is written in their passbook on the spot.
